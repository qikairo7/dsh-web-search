# AGENTS.md — Proposed Agent Notes

These notes describe proposals that are not shipped yet. Follow the [Agent Note rules](../README.md) and the [documentation standard](../../../docs/AGENTS.md).

- Use `Status: proposed` and keep `## Problem`, `## Proposal`, `## Alternatives considered`, `## Acceptance criteria`, and `## Risks`.
- Write in the future tense; do not claim the proposal is current behavior.
- When the proposal ships, move it to `implemented/`, rewrite it as `## Decision` in the present tense, and record the trade-off under `## Consequences`.
- When the proposal is declined, move it to `rejected/` and put the one-line reason on the `Status:` line.
